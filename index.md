## Professional Profile
I am a clinical psychology doctoral candidate at **UT Southwestern Medical Center**, currently serving on the **Consult-Liaison (CL) Service at Parkland Health**. My work bridges the gap between interventional psychiatry and functional health outcomes.

### Research Expertise
My research is embedded within the **Interventional Psychiatry Research Program (IPRP)** under Dr. Shawn McClintock. I focus on neuromodulation therapy outcomes, specifically regarding the support systems surrounding treatment.

* **Dissertation:** *Informing Functional and Health Outcomes of Carepartners in Electroconvulsive Therapy (ECT).*
* **Key Interests:** Consult-Liaison Psychology, Collaborative Care, Trauma-Informed Care, and Veteran Health.
